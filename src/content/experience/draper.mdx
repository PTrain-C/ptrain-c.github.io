I took this semester off from classes on purpose. A normal school schedule wasn't going to give me hands-on hardware time, and Draper would. I'm on the Microelectronics Integration & Test team in the Space Systems business area, and I work on two things.

## Analog LDO bring-up

This one came in October and it's the work I most want to be doing. Before an analog linear regulator (an LDO) gets integrated into the larger board, it gets tested on its own. I bring it up on the bench alongside the IC design team, looking at how the output responds to line transients and load transients, using Source Measure Units to drive and measure.

## Flight hardware acceptance testing

The other half is acceptance testing on 30 identical flight modules across 9 batches of 4 to 6 for a spaceflight program. I run batch 5 onward, including post-processing. The hardware design and the test plan were done before I got here. I help run it, and it's the same routine every batch.

Because the routine is the same each time, a result that looks wrong stands out. During one vibration test the script aborted. I plotted the data in MATLAB and showed the cause was a bad reading, not a bad unit, and presented that to the review board. That kind of setup-versus-hardware call comes up regularly.
